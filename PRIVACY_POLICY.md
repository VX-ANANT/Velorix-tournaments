## 2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC DATA TELEMETRY ACQUISITION
We collect only the minimum telemetry strictly necessary to administer esports tournaments, verify competitive fairness, and disburse prize winnings:

### 2.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide
1. **Identity & Contact Details**:
   - Username, Player Display Handle, Full Name.
   - Verified Mobile Phone Number and/or Email Address (via Google OAuth 2.0 or OTP verification).
   - Date of Birth (to verify 18+ age eligibility required for skill-based prize contests).
2. **Esports & Game Account Identifiers**:
   - In-Game Name (IGN) and Garena Free Fire Player UID.
   - Purpose: Direct custom room slot allocations, match verification, and leaderboard result attribution.
3. **Financial & Payout Telemetry**:
   - Virtual Payment Address (UPI ID) or registered phone number provided by you solely for withdrawing earned tournament prize winnings.
   - Transaction references and timestamps for wallet debits/credits.
   - *Note:* We do NOT collect, store, or process debit/credit card CVVs, net-banking passwords, or UPI PINs. All financial top-ups are routed securely through authorized UPI applications (FamPay, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Telemetry & Anti-Cheat Metadata
To maintain competitive integrity and detect game-breaking hacks, our automated sentry systems (including the VeloRix Autonomous Watchdog) collect:
- Device Model, Android OS Version, App Build Number.
- Hardware Identifiers (Anonymized Android Device ID) used solely to prevent multi-accounting, ban evasion, and duplicate tournament registration fraud.
- IP Address and rough network telemetry to detect illicit VPN/Proxy usage that alters matchmaking latency or circumvents regional eligibility rules.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens to deliver time-sensitive Match Room IDs and Passwords.
