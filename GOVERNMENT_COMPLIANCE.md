## 2. Distinction Between Games of Skill vs. Games of Chance

### 2.1 The "Predominant Factor" Test
Indian judicial doctrine distinguishes between gambling and skill gaming based on the **Dominant Factor Test**:
1. **Physical & Motor Dexterity**: Micro-second aiming reflexes, drag-headshot execution, movement strafing, weapon recoil compensation.
2. **Cognitive & Strategic Decision-Making**: Map zone rotations, ammunition economy, utility deployment (gloo walls, flashbangs), and team tactical coordination.
3. **Training & Experience**: Results are governed by deliberate practice, tournament experience, and mechanical execution rather than random statistical chance.

Under established Supreme Court precedents (*R.M.D. Chamarbaugwala*, *K. Satyanarayana*, *K.R. Lakshmanan*), competitions where success depends upon substantial skill do not constitute gambling or betting under state enactments.
