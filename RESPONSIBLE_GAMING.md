## 2. Key Responsible Gaming Principles

### 2.1 Play for Passion, Not For Quick Wealth
- Real-money esports tournaments carry the risk of entry fee loss if your team is outplayed in a custom room.
- Never participate with funds designated for vital life necessities (such as rent, medical expenses, groceries, or educational fees).
- Never borrow money from friends, lenders, or loan apps to play tournaments.

### 2.2 Know When to Take a Break
Competitive esports can induce intense adrenaline. Watch out for warning signs:
- Playing continuously for hours without breaks.
- Feeling anxious, irritable, or agitated when not playing.
- Attempting "chasing losses" by instantly joining higher-stake lobbies after a bad defeat.
